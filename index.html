Frequently Asked Questions — ROC Compliance
What is the due date for filing Form AOC-4 and MGT-7?
+
Form AOC-4 (financial statements) must be filed within 30 days of the Annual General Meeting (AGM), and Form MGT-7 (annual return) must be filed within 60 days of the AGM.
What happens if ROC annual filings are delayed?
+
Delayed filings attract a mandatory additional fee of ₹100 per day per form under the Companies Act, 2013, with no upper ceiling limit. Continued defaults can lead to disqualification of directors or company strike-off.
Can Advise Desk handle backlog filings for previous financial years?
+
Yes. Our CS team specializes in regularizing delayed or default filings, compounding of offences, and active status restoration for companies and LLPs.
